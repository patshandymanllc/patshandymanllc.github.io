About us

Reliable handyman services

We make it our mission to provide you with smarter, more effective service.

Pat's Handyman LLC is your trusted, local handyman service in Phoenix, Arizona. Available throughout the winter months, we specialize in a wide range of home repair and improvement services, including general repairs, carpentry, plumbing, electrical work, painting, home maintenance, and more.

Whether you need a small fix or help finishing a project, Pat's Handyman LLC is here to keep your home in top shape.
